These are original exam-board emblems fetched from official websites on 18 October 2026. They identify the collections; they do not imply endorsement of PastQ. No generated images are used.

- `jamb.png`: https://www.jamb.gov.ng/img/JAMB_logo_transparentBg.png (347 × 293), linked on https://www.jamb.gov.ng/.

Files are served locally so saved collections do not depend on third-party image requests. Display with `object-fit: contain`; do not crop or recolour the emblems.
