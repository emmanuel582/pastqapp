# FocalPoint CBT closed-test plan

Company: Wealth Consort. Support: wealthconsort@gmail.com. Intended audience: secondary-school students and older, ages 13+.

## Before the clock starts

Upload the correct-package signed AAB, complete the listing and app-content requirements, publish the closed-test release after owner review, and share its opt-in link with authorised testers. Adding email addresses to internal testing is insufficient. Record the date Console first shows at least 12 continuously opted-in closed testers. Maintain 12 or more for 14 continuous days; allow extra time if anyone leaves. Do not claim the existing 15 internal testers already satisfy this.

## Real student tasks

1. Sign up/sign in, finish onboarding and allow or decline notifications. Confirm repeated app launches do not repeatedly prompt.
2. Download WAEC/UTME materials, interrupt the network, reopen and resume. Practise downloaded questions in airplane mode.
3. Swipe within a session; verify back/edge gestures do not silently discard it. Check the digital timer, calculator movement and board resizing.
4. Finish a session; open Review answers at Question 1 and use the answer grid. Check theory question numbering and saved working.
5. Upload a permitted photo/PDF/DOCX; inspect flagged items before completion. Once the upload is accepted, close the app and inspect processing completion and its notification.
6. Complete the material once, find it in the normal library and practise it with subject/topic filters.
7. Review history and topic progress, remove an unwanted saved session/download, sign out and verify another account cannot see private work.
8. Open policy/support/account-deletion links. Test a real owner-verified deletion with a separate disposable account only after explicit authorisation. Keep the Google reviewer account active.

## Evidence

Use closed-test-log.csv for actual dated outcomes. Collect device/Android versions, build numbers, repeatable steps, screenshots with permission, severity, fixes and retests. Use anonymous tester references; do not put passwords or private papers in Git. Ask for feedback without inventing engagement or scripting positive answers.

After 14 days, answer production-access questions using this evidence: recruitment, engagement, feedback, changes made, audience/value and readiness. Google can require further testing; eligibility is not approval.

Official requirement: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465
